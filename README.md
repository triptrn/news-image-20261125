## What's in the image

- **Hero:** Two oil tankers struck by naval mines, ablaze in the Strait of Hormuz.
- **Second story:** Houthis hit Saudi Arabia's Ras Tanura oil refinery, a day
  after a strike on a Riyadh skyscraper injured 11 people.
- **Eight numbered stories:** markets plunge across Asia and the US (KOSPI
  −6.16%, Dow's first close above 50,000); Huawei convicted in a US court;
  Ukraine hits two Rosneft refineries; Gaza; Sudan; COP31 climate talks
  collapse; the Australia–Solomon Islands security pact; and Shenzhou-24
  docking with Tiangong, carrying Pakistan's first astronaut.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only) copies the fixed `entry.png` (1024×1024,
PNG) byte-for-byte to `/output/entry.png`. Two runs produce identical bytes.
